Piano chord chart: every major and minor chord

A major chord is a root, a major third (4 semitones up) and a fifth (7 semitones up). A minor chord lowers the middle note by one semitone.

RootMajorMinor

DD - F# - AD - F - A
D#D# - G - A#D# - F# - A#
EE - G# - BE - G - B
FF - A - CF - G# - C
F#F# - A# - C#F# - A - C#
GG - B - DG - A# - D
G#G# - C - D#G# - B - D#
AA - C# - EA - C - E
A#A# - D - FA# - C# - F
