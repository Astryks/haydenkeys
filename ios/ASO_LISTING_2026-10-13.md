## Description (1401/4000 chars)

Learn piano for free with Ginger and Pepper, two cats who make practice fun.
250+ lessons and 240+ songs, with chords for every section and falling notes to play along.
No ads. No account. No paywall. Just sit down at your piano or keyboard and start.

WHY HAYDEN KEYS
Piano apps often charge a monthly fee before you play a single song. Hayden Keys is completely free. If you enjoy it, there's an optional tip jar, and that's all.

LEARN AT YOUR OWN PACE
• 250+ lessons to guide you from your first notes onward
• Teacher videos that show you how to play
• Inspiration videos to keep you motivated
• Streaks to help you practise a little every day

PLAY 240+ SONGS
• Chords for every section of every song
• Falling-note play-along so you can see what's coming
• Upload a song and get its chords

WAIT FOR ME, ON A REAL PIANO
Wait for me mode listens to your real piano and waits until you play the right note, so you learn without rushing.
No piano nearby? Use the on-screen keyboard.

TRAIN YOUR EAR
• Chord Ear Gym: learn to recognise 24 chords by sound
• Guess the song: upload a recording and the app tells you which song it is, then you can jump straight into learning it

GREAT FOR BEGINNERS
• Friendly mascots Ginger and Pepper
• No sign-up, so you can start in seconds
• Works with a real piano or the on-screen keyboard

Hayden Keys is made by Astryks. Free, no ads, no account, with an optional tip jar if you'd like to support development.

More at haydenkeys.com
