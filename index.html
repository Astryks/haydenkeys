Frequently asked questions

Is Hayden Keys free?

Yes. Hayden Keys is completely free, with no ads, no subscription and no paywall. There is an optional tip jar in the iPhone and iPad app if you want to support development, and a tip unlocks nothing extra.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, so you can start in seconds. Your progress and streaks stay on your own device.

What are the best beginner piano chords?

Start with G, D, Em and C. Hayden Keys' first lesson teaches these as the 1 - 5 - 6 - 4 pattern in G major, the I - V - vi - IV loop behind a huge number of pop songs.

Do I need a real piano or keyboard?

Hayden Keys works with a real piano or keyboard, and Wait for me listens to the notes you play. No piano nearby? Use the on-screen keyboard.

Is my microphone audio recorded or uploaded?

No. The microphone is optional and the sound is analysed on your device. It is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Do the song pages include lyrics?

No. Song pages show the key and chord names only, never lyrics.
