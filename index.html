Hayden Keys

Learn any song on the piano for free!

Hayden Keys is a free app to learn piano for beginners: 250+ short piano lessons, 240+ songs with chords for every section, falling-note play-along, and Wait for me, which listens to your real piano or keyboard. Learn chords, both hands, reading notes and jazz tricks. No ads, no account.
